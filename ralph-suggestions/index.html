What This Is

Debugging Human Communication applies the scientific method to real conversations. Over time, your conversations improve because you treat them as systems, not personality flaws.

Who This Is For

If you're a technical leader who is having trouble breaking through, this is for you.

This work is designed for people who:

  • • Think in systems, logic, and cause and effect
  • • Are tired of generic “soft skills” advice
  • • Want practical improvement, not theory

Common Situations:

  • • Meetings where your ideas are ignored
  • • Stakeholders who misunderstand your intent
  • • Feedback that lands badly
  • • Conflict that keeps repeating
