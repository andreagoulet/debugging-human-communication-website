# DHC Program Hero Video — Script

Teleprompter-ready. Sells the program (not the workshop), CTA is booking a call.
Target runtime: ~2 minutes at a conversational pace.

---

Hi there. I'm Andrea Goulet, and if you don't know who I am, I've taught over a hundred thousand people in the software industry how to communicate so their conversations flow, their messages land, and their influence grows.

Because a lot of technical professionals struggle to be really understood. They're frustrated because they're kind and they're compassionate and they work really hard, but they just don't know how to approach human communication with the same clarity and confidence that they have when they code.

And that's why I created the Debugging Human Communication Program.

This isn't a course with modules and a certificate at the end. Everything in the program is anchored around experiments and community. You get access to:

- Two "communication labs" every week. In these virtual group coaching sessions, you'll work through communication challenges with other software professionals who understand the nuances of the industry. 
- A private Discord community filled with software professionals who think the same way you do, so you don't have to dumb things down or pretend to be someone you're not.
- Book clubs to help you learn with others. And don't worry — there's no pressure to read the sections. We have a "come as you are" culture with these.

On top of this, I also work with a small number of private coaching clients who are ready to really focus and get results even faster. 

Debugging Human Communication is different from every other communication training you've come across. Because I don't give empty advice you can't implement, like "read the room." No, I show you specifically how you can refactor human conversations the exact same way that you refactor your code.

And this isn't theory. People I've coached have hit number one on Hacker News. They've gotten surprise bonuses. They've been paid thousands of dollars for workshops they used to give away for free. They've gotten new headcount approved in the middle of a hiring freeze. They've transformed the dread of breaking the ice into joy of human connection. 

You can check out some of the stories in the testimonials below.

So if this resonates, think about what's at stake. Think about the cost of doing nothing — struggling to start, sustain, and stop conversations. Not getting your projects funded. Getting passed over for jobs or promotions. Or having industry-changing ideas and not knowing how to share them. If even one of those conversations went differently, that could change your whole trajectory.

So here's the next step. Book a call with me. And this isn't a sales call — it's a fit call. We'll talk about where your communication is breaking down and whether the program is the right fit for you. And if I don't think it is, I'll tell you. So you really don't have anything to lose by having the conversation.

You can debug your human communication, and I can show you how. The link to book time with me is right below this video, and I can't wait to start the conversation.
