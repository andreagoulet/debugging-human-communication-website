### The Method

> "Here's the core insight that shapes how we work together: you don't learn communication by reading books or sitting in a classroom listening to a lecture. You learn it the same way you've learned everything that matters in your career — by running experiments, measuring what happens, and iterating."

> "Debugging Human Communication is a community of practice, not a curriculum. Everything we do is built on the scientific method, because it's the surest way to discover what works."

### What's Included

> "Here's what membership in the community includes:"

- **Onboarding Session** — when you join, you'll start with a 90-minute goal-setting workshop with me. I want to make sure that you're really clear on what you're working towards because knowing what success looks like is essential to running successful experiments.
- **Labs** — Labs are where the magic happens. They're the steady rhythm where good intentions turn into actionable experiments that you can test immediately. We meet twice a week: Wednesdays from 11am - noon and Thursdays from 3-4pm eastern. I attend as many as I can; when I can't (sick, vacation, corporate training), lab assistants step in to facilitate. The meetings run like this: first few minutes are spent getting clear on what problem you want to solve. Everyone shares and we vote on which ones to focus on. We take about 8-10 minutes for a discussion, then the person who submitted the problem defines a specific experiment to test. We usually get through 3-4 experiments per session and I keep track of who has gone so everyone gets a chance to participate. When there's a big breakthrough — you've got a group of people who are excited to celebrate with you.
- **Thought Experiment Workshops** — This is where I share what the science says. While I have over 20 years of experience as a professional communicator, it's the research, not my intuition, that drives what I share. You can't get this content anywhere else. I custom develop these workshops based on the challenges members are grappling with, and I design experiment templates that go with each topic. Sessions take place every other Monday from 4pm - 5:30pm eastern.
- **Online Community** — connect with other members whenever you need to. This is where we stay in touch and celebrate wins, swap experiments, ask for support, and build relationships. Members can host their own events. We've done things like book clubs and deliberate practice groups. Prepping for a hard conversation, stuck drafting a post, debriefing a meeting that just happened? The community is there between sessions.
- **Library** — every Thought Experiment is recorded and added to a members-only library, along with additional resources and materials. If you can't attend live or want to revisit a pattern, the archive is there.

> "What's NOT in here, on purpose: there's no 20-hour course to slog through. There's no certificate. There's no pre-planned curriculum to march through. This is a working practice built around real experiments that address real challenges from real people like you."

This is how you get to the green and not the red. Not by passively ingesting more information, but by taking an active role and experimenting in community."

### Pricing

> "The membership is **$5,000 for six months. After that, it's $500 per month as long as you want to continue**."

<p class="stage-direction">Let that land. Pause.</p>

> "Here's what I want you to compare that to. Think about what changes when you have access to high-quality experiments that help you address the challenges you've already identified. You've already asked yourself what would change if you could consistently get the right message to the right person. Take a moment and consider the impact this community can have on your situation."

**And since we like data, let's make this concrete with some numbers:**

- How much more would you earn from a promotion, raise, or bonus. $10K? $20? $100K+?
- Maybe you're interested in public speaking. Clients I work with have gone from speaking for free to charging $5,000+ per engagement.
- If you own a business, how much more would you earn from closing more deals and landing better clients? $50K? $100K? $500K+?
- It might be an investor conversation. If the right message hit the right person the first time: that could be the difference between funded and "interesting, keep us posted"

> "Compare six months of membership in a community where you're not just sitting in a classroom — you're experimenting in the real world, and you've got the support you need to keep going when things get hard and the expertise to design experiments based on real research instead of guesswork. You could spend $5000 on a certification program or about a dozen sessions of executive coaching, but is that giving you the most value to help you get from where you are today to what you've defined as success?"
>
> "If even one of the outcomes we talked about today happens — the promotion, the talk, the project that gets funded — the membership pays for itself many times over."

### The Feedback Call

> "You can't buy anything today. I'm not going to share any links for you to sign up because I want today to be about value. What I will share is a link to my calendar. I want to hear about your specific situation, understand what you're trying to amplify, and help you figure out if this is the right fit right now. If it is, great — I'll walk you through how to get started. If it's not, I'll tell you that too, and I'll point you toward what might work better."
