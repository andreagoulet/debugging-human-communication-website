Call on people to share their biggest gap areas.

**Normalize:** *"Nobody in this room is green on everything — and you shouldn't be. Anyone claiming green across all 9 would be overstating. And this changes based on the situation, too. When things are uncertain, new, or we're stressed — all of that impacts our ability to communicate. The point is to know **specifically** where you are, so you know specifically where to experiment."*

**Bridge:** But here's the challenge — experimenting alone can be really hard. One person said that when she tried, it felt like performing heart surgery on herself. That's because you're so close to the problem. So that's why I created the Debugging Human Communication community. Is it okay with you if I share what I've designed?"
