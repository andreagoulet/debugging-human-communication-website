### This is a working laboratory

> "This isn't a lecture and it's not a pep talk. For the next two hours, **think of this as a laboratory** — a place where you are going to use the scientific method to **define a problem, explore the variables, and leave with an experiment** that you've designed to help you solve whatever communication challenge you've identified. This is about getting real clarity on **how to get the right message to the right person, every time**."

### How to get the most out of this

> "**Grab something to write with**. This is a highly interactive experience."

> "**Limit distractions as best you can.** Take a minute to turn off distracting notifications so you can go deep on problem solving the way you like to."

>"**Share in the chat.** There are a lot of amazing people in this room who are going through similar challenges. Share your thoughts, ideas, and questions there as much as you'd like."

>"**Use the hand raise feature.** There will be times where I invite people to share their experiences. When I do and you want to talk, click the hand raise feature at the bottom of your screen so I can call on you." 

>"**Give yourself permission to be honest.** This workshop isn't about me giving you all the answers. It's about me giving you a framework to diagose challenges yourself and discover your own path forward." 

### What you'll leave with

> "By the end of the workshop, you'll have a clear picture of **which communication challenge will give you the most leverage** — and your **first communication experiment that you can start implementing immediately**. Is everyone ready?"
